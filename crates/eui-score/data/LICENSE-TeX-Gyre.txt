Letter outlines for the text `score()` prints: titles, composers, tempo
marks. In thousandths of an em, y down, the baseline at 0; `a` is the
advance and `f` the canvas fill commands (03 §1.1, kind 5, without the
kind and the colour). Whole numbers, so the file carries no "0." twice a point.

GENERATED by `cargo run -p xtask -- letters <regular.otf> <bold.otf> <italic.otf> <licence>`
from TeX Gyre Schola, the face LilyPond 2.24 sets its text in. Do not
edit by hand. This is a derived work of that font: a subset of its
letters, re-encoded, under a name of its own as the GUST Font License
asks of derived works.
